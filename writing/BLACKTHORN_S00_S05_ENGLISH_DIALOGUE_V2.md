# BLACKTHORN — S00–S05 · English Dialogue V2

**Editorial draft — not yet active on the website.** Expanded from V1 for warmer friendships, lived-in pacing and individual voices. The original Blackthorn V18 scene IDs, evidence, route conditions, encounter order and phone choices remain authoritative. Conversations here are proposed, not newly established mystery canon. All dialogue is in English.

**Performance key:** Names identify separate eventual voice actors/voice IDs. Bracketed actions are **not spoken**. All optional player replies are labeled CHOICE; don't play them automatically. Do not reveal an individual phone route to the other two players.

## S00 — THE VEILED MOON
**Place:** Blackthorn Academy exterior. **Art:** rainy academy exterior.

[Rainwater gathers between the stones of the approach. The building stands quiet against a pale morning sky. A single brief opening narration; do not invent a historian's dialogue.]

**NARRATOR:** “Four centuries ago, Blackthorn's founding families made their winter pact beneath a moon hidden by clouds.”

[The view rises toward the old windows.]

**NARRATOR:** “On the final evening of Founders' Week, the masks return. Not to make everyone anonymous, but to remind them that a reputation is not necessarily the truth.”

[Title. The opening lingers for a breath before Suite 417.]

**Voice:** a separate narrator, not any character's eventual voice. Keep this rare throughout the game.

## S01 — SUITE 417, MORNING
**Place:** Suite 417. **Art:** guest suite getting ready. **Props:** exactly three personal invitations, programme, campus map, closed mask boxes, window; optional group-photo action. **Phone:** P001 system notice at 08:02: outfits delivered at 17:30, invitations individually registered.

[Someone has opened the window a little. Cool air moves the edge of a folded campus map on the desk. Maxine is sitting cross-legged on a chair, trying to arrange papers without making a bigger pile. Sam is half unpacked. Florine has the map the wrong way round.]

**MAXINE:** “That is not what I thought sharing a room would look like.”

**SAM:** “It's been twenty minutes.”

**MAXINE:** “And somehow every surface is already full.”

**FLORINE:** “There are three of us.”

**MAXINE:** “I am starting to notice.”

[Florine turns the map around, then pauses.]

**SAM:** “You had it upside down.”

**FLORINE:** “I was checking whether you'd notice.”

**SAM:** “Of course.”

**FLORINE:** “Okay, fine. I was looking for the dining hall.”

[Sam comes over rather than calling directions from across the room.]

**SAM:** “Here. That's the main stair, I think. Unless this is the main stair.”

**MAXINE:** “You're both inspiring confidence.”

**SAM:** “I did say 'I think.'”

[Maxine discovers three envelopes beneath the programme.]

**MAXINE:** “Wait. These are addressed separately.”

**FLORINE:** “Are you surprised they know our names?”

**MAXINE:** “No. I just thought we'd have one room invitation.”

**SAM:** “It would be quite a small invitation for three names.”

**MAXINE:** “I could have asked without commentary.”

[Maxine sets the envelopes where each person can reach her own. Her tone softens.]

**MAXINE:** “Here. Yours, yours. Don't lose them, please.”

**FLORINE:** “You say that like I lose things.”

**SAM:** “You spent ten minutes looking for your keys yesterday.”

**FLORINE:** “They were in my coat.”

**SAM:** “Which you were wearing.”

[Florine starts to protest, reconsiders, and opens her envelope instead.]

**FLORINE:** “Fair.”

[The boxes containing their masks sit unopened on the dresser.]

**SAM:** “Are we meant to do something with those now?”

**MAXINE:** “No. Tonight.”

**SAM:** “Right. I know tonight. I just wasn't sure if there was a fitting or something.”

**FLORINE:** “I hope not. We're already doing enough preparation for one party.”

**MAXINE:** “It's Founders' Week. Apparently the party is the preparation.”

[Florine reads the programme while Sam puts a loose shirt back in her bag.]

**FLORINE:** “Breakfast. Founders' History. Archive Methods.”

**SAM:** “We've been here one night and they're giving us homework?”

**MAXINE:** “It's a programme, not a threat.”

**SAM:** “You're the one who read it like a deadline.”

[An ordinary, slightly awkward silence. Florine folds the map with some difficulty.]

**FLORINE:** “I'm glad we're here together.”

[Neither of the others answers immediately; the change in tone is unexpected.]

**SAM:** “Me too.”

**MAXINE:** “Even if this room is going to look like a disaster for the entire week.”

**FLORINE:** “Especially then.”

**MAXINE:** “No. Definitely not especially then.”

[They exchange a small smile. A notification sounds, pulling them back into the morning.]

**PHONE P001 — Guest Services, ALL (text, not voiced as a person in the room):** “The Founders' Week outfits will be delivered to Suite 417 at 17:30. Personal invitations have been registered individually.”

**SAM:** “Good. We don't have to think about clothes until later.”

**MAXINE:** “We still need to find the lecture hall.”

**FLORINE:** “I have a map.”

**SAM:** “We noticed.”

[Maxine picks up her invitation and puts it somewhere safe. This does **not** automatically mean either other player has done so.]

**MAXINE:** “Breakfast first?”

**SAM:** “Please.”

**FLORINE:** “And coffee.”

**MAXINE:** “That wasn't a question.”

**Optional player hotspots:** INVITATIONS / PROGRAMME / MAP / CLOSED MASK BOXES / WINDOW / GROUP PHOTO. Their appearance is not a mandatory choice or proof of a photo being taken. Preserve `map_opened` for Sam's later Nate branch.

**Pacing:** They can be affectionate without performing constant banter. Let short silences breathe. No ominous line is needed.

## S02 — BREAKFAST, CLASS AND THE GRAND LIBRARY
**Place sequence:** Dining Hall → North Lecture Hall → Grand Library. **Main art:** `BG_North_Lecture_Hall.png`; use short transitions rather than showing the lecture-hall picture as if it were also a dining room. **Canon:** Celeste Rook's two explanations about masks and procession are choice-dependent; don't speak them twice.

### A. Dining Hall

[They find a place at the end of a shared table. Sam has her programme folded into a narrow strip; Maxine holds the edge down so it doesn't spring open.]

**SAM:** “I slept terribly.”

**MAXINE:** “The mattress?”

**SAM:** “No. I kept waking up convinced I'd missed something.”

**FLORINE:** “You haven't missed anything. We got here yesterday.”

**SAM:** “That's what made it annoying.”

**MAXINE:** “You could have said. I was awake for ages too.”

**SAM:** “Why?”

**MAXINE:** “Couldn't switch off.”

[Florine looks up from her breakfast.]

**FLORINE:** “Are we going to be exhausted before the week even starts?”

**MAXINE:** “Possibly. Eat.”

[They do, for a moment. Not every mouthful needs dialogue.]

**SAM:** “Do either of you know where we're meant to sit for history?”

**FLORINE:** “On chairs, I assume.”

**MAXINE:** “Florine.”

**FLORINE:** “I don't know. I have the room number.”

**SAM:** “That would've been a much better answer.”

### B. North Lecture Hall

[Cut to students finding seats. Professor Celeste Rook straightens a few pages, waits for the room to settle, and starts without an elaborate greeting.]

**ROOK:** “We're going to talk about traditions today. Not the nice brochure version. The practical one.”

[Someone near the back shuts a bag too loudly.]

**ROOK:** “Thank you.”

[She looks briefly at the students. It is impossible to tell whether she means it.]

**ROOK:** “Traditions rarely begin because someone wants a mystery. More often they're practical arrangements that outlive the problem they solved.”

[Maxine writes part of the sentence down. Florine notices.]

**FLORINE (quietly):** “You're taking notes already?”

**MAXINE (quietly):** “I'm allowed.”

**FLORINE:** “I didn't say you weren't.”

**ROOK:** “Is there a question?”

[Florine straightens, caught.]

**FLORINE:** “Not yet.”

**ROOK:** “Then let's see how long that lasts.”

**PLAYER SHARED CHOICE — select ONE and show only its matching answer:**

**A. ASK ABOUT MASKS**
**FLORINE or chosen player:** “Why masks, originally? Was it always just for the ceremony?”
**ROOK:** “No. They marked roles and access. Later, people started seeing more symbolism in them.”
**SAM (quietly, if this choice played):** “That actually makes sense.”
**MAXINE:** “It's a little less dramatic than the programme.”

**B. ASK ABOUT THE PROCESSION**
**Chosen player:** “Why does the procession have such a specific route?”
**ROOK:** “A route is logistics first. Ceremony comes afterwards. Ask any organiser who's had to move a crowd through a building.”
**FLORINE (quietly, if this choice played):** “I suppose the corridors don't get wider just because it's tradition.”

**C. LET THE LESSON CONTINUE**
[No one interrupts. Rook moves on with the class. Do not award a clue from either question.]

[After the branch, Rook puts down her notes.]

**ROOK:** “You don't have to admire a custom to understand why it exists. That's all I'm asking.”

**SAM (quietly, to the others):** “I like her.”

**MAXINE:** “You like anyone who says something sensible.”

**SAM:** “That's not true.”

**FLORINE:** “No, it is a bit true.”

**SAM:** “I can hear you.”

### C. Grand Library / Archive Methods

[Chairs scrape across the floor as another class takes its place. The library is quieter than the lecture hall. For a moment, the girls are simply glad to sit down.]

**FLORINE:** “I thought that would be more boring.”

**MAXINE:** “You asked her a question.”

**FLORINE:** “I thought I'd lose track halfway through.”

**SAM:** “I almost did. Then she started talking about how people actually use the rooms.”

**MAXINE:** “It helps when a lesson is about something real.”

[The next class begins. Fade out before inventing any unapproved archival clue.]

**Exit S03.** No extra documents, possessions or evidence are introduced here.

## S03 — SUITE 417, LATER · GETTING READY
**Place:** Suite 417. **Art:** Candlelit Masquerade Dressing Room. **Known timing:** P001 outfit delivery 17:30, Mila's P002 reminder 19:35, departure 20:14. After 20:14 **do not display an absolute Academy clock**.

[Later. Outfit bags hang from the wardrobe. The invitation envelopes have moved around the room; the masks are out of their boxes. Someone has left a hairbrush in the middle of the bed.]

**MAXINE:** “Whose is this?”

**SAM:** “Mine.”

**MAXINE:** “Can it be not there?”

**SAM:** “I was using it.”

**MAXINE:** “You're not using it now.”

[Sam moves it, without turning this into an argument.]

**SAM:** “There.”

**MAXINE:** “Thank you.”

[Florine checks the fastening on her outfit in the mirror.]

**FLORINE:** “Does this sit strangely?”

**SAM:** “Turn around.”

[Florine does. Sam takes a second to look properly.]

**SAM:** “No. I think you're just not used to it.”

**FLORINE:** “I don't feel like myself in it.”

**MAXINE:** “You're allowed to look different for one evening.”

**FLORINE:** “I know. I just—”

**SAM:** “You look good. You don't have to feel comfortable immediately.”

**FLORINE:** “Thanks.”

[Maxine tries to pin something in place, stops, and starts over.]

**FLORINE:** “Want a hand?”

**MAXINE:** “I've got it.”

[It slips again.]

**MAXINE:** “Actually. Yes.”

[Florine helps. It takes longer than either expected. They don't need a joke to fill the silence.]

**SAM:** “Can I ask you something?”

**MAXINE:** “Depends.”

**SAM:** “Do you ever get nervous before things like this?”

**MAXINE:** “Yes.”

**SAM:** “You never look it.”

**MAXINE:** “That's generally the idea.”

[Sam considers this. Maxine catches her reflection beside Sam's in the mirror.]

**MAXINE:** “Are you nervous?”

**SAM:** “A little. It's the people, I think. Not knowing anyone.”

**FLORINE:** “You know us.”

**SAM:** “That's not exactly what I meant.”

**FLORINE:** “I know. But we'll still be there.”

[Maxine puts the fastening down, satisfied.]

**MAXINE:** “And if it gets unbearable, we can leave early.”

**SAM:** “Can we?”

**MAXINE:** “We have legs.”

**FLORINE:** “I vote we at least see the ballroom first.”

**SAM:** “Agreed.”

[The three start gathering their things. Sam searches the dressing table.]

**SAM:** “Has anyone seen my invitation?”

**FLORINE:** “Again?”

**SAM:** “Don't start.”

**MAXINE:** “Under the programme.”

[Sam lifts it and retrieves the envelope.]

**SAM:** “You could have told me.”

**MAXINE:** “You hadn't asked yet.”

**SAM:** “I just did.”

**MAXINE:** “And I told you.”

[A message arrives.]

**PHONE P002 — Mila Vermeer, ALL (text):** “Just a reminder, because someone always forgets: bring the actual invitations. They're checking them downstairs.”

[Sam holds her envelope up.]

**SAM:** “Apparently Mila knows me.”

**MAXINE:** “She knows everyone.”

**FLORINE:** “Masks?”

[They check. Do not imply every player has selected an accessory or taken a photo unless those options were used.]

**MAXINE:** “Here.”

**SAM:** “Here.”

**FLORINE:** “Good. Let's go.”

[The Academy clock can show **20:14** as they depart. Hide it after this cue.]

**Optional interactions:** ROOM HOTSPOTS, ACCESSORY CHOICE, GROUP PHOTO. No automatic assumption about choices.

## S05 — FOUNDERS' EVENING, GRAND HALL
**Place:** Grand Hall. **Art:** `extra-original-photos/3B. Grand Ballroom.webp`. **Canon prop:** a dark document case under Celeste Rook's arm. **Canon speakers:** Aldous Veyne and Celeste Rook; avoid premature revelations. **Interaction choices:** ask Veyne about Founders' Week, respond to Rook, own words.

[Warm light falls over the wooden galleries. The music is loud enough that the three have to move closer to hear one another. They find each other just inside the hall.]

**SAM:** “Oh, there you are.”

**FLORINE:** “I said I'd be inside.”

**SAM:** “You did.”

**MAXINE:** “Can we move? This is the worst place to stand.”

[They weave towards the edge of the crowd. For a few seconds they simply take in the room.]

**SAM:** “That's beautiful.”

**FLORINE:** “The ceiling?”

**SAM:** “All of it, really.”

**MAXINE:** “Okay. I take back half of what I said about the dress code.”

**FLORINE:** “Only half?”

**MAXINE:** “Don't make me reconsider.”

[Sam tucks a loose strand of hair behind her ear. Her eyes keep moving from balcony to balcony.]

**FLORINE:** “You okay?”

**SAM:** “Yeah. Just looking.”

**MAXINE:** “We can stand here a minute. Nobody's making us do anything yet.”

[Florine shifts her mask. Maxine notices the movement.]

**MAXINE:** “Is it uncomfortable?”

**FLORINE:** “A little. It keeps slipping.”

**MAXINE:** “Here.”

[She adjusts the strap without making a performance of helping.]

**FLORINE:** “Thanks.”

**MAXINE:** “Better?”

**FLORINE:** “Much.”

[Professor Aldous Veyne passes nearby, addressing a late guest.]

**VEYNE:** “If you're going to arrive late, at least do it in a way somebody remembers.”

[He returns to the conversation around him. One of the three catches the rest of his remark.]

**VEYNE:** “Blackthorn adores its protocols most when nobody asks which problem they were supposed to solve.”

**SAM (quietly):** “He sounds as though he's been asked that before.”

**MAXINE:** “He probably has.”

[Celeste Rook joins them with a dark document case under her arm. She does not offer to open it, set it down or discuss it.]

**ROOK:** “Welcome to Blackthorn. Don't believe anyone who tells you Founders' Week is usually calmer.”

**FLORINE:** “Was it really that bad last year?”

**ROOK:** “Different problems. Similar number of people.”

[She gives a small, polite smile. Before she leaves, she checks that the entrance remains clear. There is no portentous close-up of the case.]

**MAXINE:** “I don't think she was joking.”

**SAM:** “I don't think she needed to be.”

**FLORINE:** “Can we actually enjoy the evening before we start analysing everyone?”

[Maxine looks at her, amused.]

**MAXINE:** “You're the one analysing them.”

**FLORINE:** “I asked one question.”

**SAM:** “You do ask very specific questions.”

[Florine opens her mouth to argue, then laughs once and shakes her head.]

**FLORINE:** “Fine. Who wants something to drink?”

**MAXINE:** “Now you're speaking my language.”

**SAM:** “Just give me a second.”

[She looks back up at the balconies. Florine waits; Maxine does too. No need to add a mystery wink. The players should have time to experience the grandness of the hall.]

## Editorial/implementation acceptance checks

1. **Natural conversation:** no compulsory joke in every exchange; unremarkable sentences and pauses are deliberate. Performances should be conversational rather than dramatic monologues.
2. **Personality:** hiding the speaker labels should still make the three friends and three first-contact NPCs distinguishable, but not because of rigid catchphrases.
3. **S00:** Narrator is separate and rare. The historical framing remains V18-authorised, with no invented pact facts.
4. **S01:** Do not force interaction choices; keep invitations individual, boxes initially closed, programme and map present.
5. **S02:** The two Rook answers must be **exclusive interactive branches** rather than fixed exposition duplicated in a branch. This document is written that way.
6. **S03:** P001 says delivery at 17:30; P002 reminder at 19:35; leave at 20:14; the clock disappears afterward.
7. **S04:** 3 private routes and no projector spoilers. Sam's map/no-map openings are mutually exclusive. Maxine's Theo reaction changes are proposed and must be checked.
8. **S05:** Rook's case exists and is not interpreted for the players; Veyne's optional Founders' Week answer is not delivered automatically.
9. **Voice acting:** only create recordings after approving exact line text. Character-to-voice identity remains fixed across future scenes; voice acting must allow genuine softness, irritation, neutrality and humour from one actor.
10. **Not live yet:** No code/UI/audio files should be changed based solely on this review draft. Check these dialogue proposals against canonical choices before integration.

*File intention:* V2 deliberately gives S01 and S03 much more room, makes S02 a small lived-in school day, and stops S04 from becoming shared exposition. The exact duration of the scene can still be cut after an aloud reading and a three-player rehearsal.
